02 / Our approach

The business first.
The technology
comes next.

Understand how the work happens. Find the most useful place to start. Then build confidence with something your team can actually use.

Your
business
People
Information
Systems
  1. 01

    Map the business

    Look at the people, processes and systems involved, and where work gets held up or repeated.

  2. 02

    Choose the priorities

    Identify what is worth improving and where AI is a sensible fit. Agree what useful progress would look like.

  3. 03

    Start small. Make it useful.

    Begin with a focused improvement, a suitable tool or a small integration that solves a real problem.

  4. 04

    Connect what adds value

    Link the information, tools and next steps where it makes the work easier. Keep the setup proportionate to the need.

  5. 05

    Test it with your people

    Try it on real work, check the output and refine it with the people using it. Make review and responsibility clear.
